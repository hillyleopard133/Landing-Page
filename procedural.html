Procedural Generation

Overview

The temple system uses grid-based procedural generation to create randomly structured layouts within defined constraints. Generation begins by placing basic rooms to form the core grid, expanding outward from a central spawn point in branching paths.

Once the base layout is created, it is validated against constraints such as minimum and maximum room counts. If valid, special rooms are assigned: relic and boss rooms are placed in the furthest locations from the spawn, treasure rooms are distributed among remaining spaces, and enemies are then spawned throughout the temple.

Find and activate all relics within the temple to unlock the final boss room. Defeat the boss to unlock the elemental power associated with that temple. Progress through increasingly difficult temples as new abilities are gained.
