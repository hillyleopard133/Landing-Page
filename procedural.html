Procedural Generation

Temple Gameplay

Overview

The temple system uses grid-based procedural generation to create randomly structured layouts within defined constraints. Generation begins by placing basic rooms to form the core grid, expanding outward from a central spawn point in branching paths.

Once the base layout is created, it is validated against constraints such as minimum and maximum room counts. If valid, special rooms are assigned: relic and boss rooms are placed in the furthest locations from the spawn, treasure rooms are distributed among remaining spaces, and enemies are then spawned throughout the temple.

Find and activate all relics within the temple to unlock the final boss room. Defeat the boss to unlock the elemental power associated with that temple. Progress through increasingly difficult temples as new abilities are gained.

Temple Systems

Procedural Layout Generation

Temples are generated on a grid using branching paths from a central spawn room. Layouts are randomised within defined constraints to ensure varied and valid dungeon structures. Rooms are instantiated using prefabs during generation to construct the final temple layout.

Special Room Placement

Once a valid layout is created, relic, boss, and treasure rooms are placed within the generated structure based on distance from the spawn and available room slots.

Relic Activation

Relics must be activated throughout the temple to unlock the final boss encounter and progress through the temple. The amount of relics required to unlock the boss room is displayed below the minimap.

Temple Completion

Defeating the boss completes the temple and unlocks a new elemental ability tied to that region. The player can then progress through the exit in the boss room.

Treasure

Treasure chests are distributed throughout temples and tracked as a found/total count in the UI. Each chest contains a random item selected from a predefined loot pool, rewarding exploration and full dungeon completion.
